Our Year

Events

Shivasya is active through the festival season, May to October, plus community-service events year-round. Here is what our year looks like.

Ganeshotsav

Our signature performance, at the Hindu Center of Charlotte.

Festival of India

A long-running celebration in Uptown Charlotte.

Shiv Jayanti Food Drive

Every February, with the Charlotte Marathi Mandal and Second Harvest Food Bank.

Humanity Blood Drive

Every August, around Independence Day, with CMM and the Hindu Center.

Festivals & more

Harrisburg Multicultural Festival, Brahmotsav, Sai Temple, Gandhi Jayanti, and others.

Charity runs & seva

5K and 10K runs, Diwali food collection, and volunteering when the community needs us.
