Who we are

A family of drummers, dancers, and volunteers

Founded in 2013 by a handful of families, Shivasya has grown into an 80+ member crew. We perform at festivals across the Carolinas and serve our community through food drives, blood drives, and youth mentorship. Everyone is welcome — all ages, all skill levels.
