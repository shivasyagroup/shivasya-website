The Crew

Who We Are

There's a place for everyone in the pathak. No experience is needed. All ages are welcome, men and women alike.

The roles you can play:

DholTashaZanj (cymbals)Taal (gong)LezimLathi KathiTutariDhwaj (flag)Abdagir

How it started

The group was founded in 2013. The founders were Shantanu Wadodkar, Kedar Hinge, Dajiba Patil, Sachin Varule, Pramod Hole, and Poonam Kulkarni. Nitu Patil led the lezim activity. Many have shaped Shivasya since. Among them are Pratapsinh Patil, Supriya Bhor, Mahesh Bhor, and Jeet Hiremath.

Today the crew is 80+ strong and still growing. We practice twice a week, on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Practice runs from 7:00 to 8:30 PM, May through October. We meet at the Hindu Center of Charlotte.
