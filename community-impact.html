Food Drive

Every February, around Shiv Jayanti, we hold our annual Food Drive. We partner with the Charlotte Marathi Mandal and Second Harvest Food Bank. Second Harvest notes that every $1 donated provides 7 pounds of food. Counting cash gifts that way, a single drive has delivered more than 20,000 pounds of food to families in need.

Humanity Blood Donation Drive

Every August, around Independence Day, we hold our Humanity Blood Donation Drive. We partner with the Charlotte Marathi Mandal and the Hindu Center of Charlotte. A recent drive brought together 110 donors.

More ways we give back

We also join charity 5K and 10K runs and Diwali food collection. We volunteer during community events and natural calamities. Our students earn documented volunteer and service hours — one young member reached 100 hours, largely through Shivasya.

Our work has been featured in the Independent Tribune and esakal.

Youth Service & Leadership

Our young members earn documented volunteer and service hours through Shivasya's seva — food drives, blood drives, charity runs, teaching, and community events — and many step up to lead. We're proud to celebrate them.
